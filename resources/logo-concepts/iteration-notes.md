# Iteration after Gemini round 1

Retained 01 and 10. Concepts 02–09 were removed from selection and kept only as design history.

01: joined horizontal dividers to the fold apex and vertical stem; symmetric 1.5-unit insets; normalized 64×48 container; added a 6-unit-stroke micro variant for 16–24 px.

10: moved flap apex to x=43 to align with the offset vertical partition; closed horizontal seams. Deliberate asymmetric envelope remains a secondary candidate.

05 was eliminated because its silhouette is less immediately recognizable and a separate filled mark would add inconsistency.
